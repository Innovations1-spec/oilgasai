The Story

Built in the field.
Not on a whiteboard.

OilGasAI grew out of two years of hands-on methane research at Colorado State University's METEC facility — a DOE-funded controlled-release testing site in Fort Collins, Colorado.

2021–2023
Field deployment. Designed, built, and deployed the AMMMU — Autonomous Mobile Methane Measurement Unit — at METEC. Integrated TGS2600/2611 MOx sensors, DHT22, Airmar weather station, and Raspberry Pi data logger on solar power.
2024
ML calibration published. Developed a Random Forest regression pipeline that brings low-cost sensor accuracy to within ±0.8 ppm of reference-grade analyzers. Published in Atmosphere (MDPI), cited 9 times independently.
2025–2026
OilGasAI Model Alpha trained. Fine-tuned Llama 3.1 70B on 21,429 domain-specific pairs using 10,000 GPU hours on CINECA's Leonardo HPC — allocated by UNDP. Published on Hugging Face. Platform deployed.
2026 →
U.S. commercial launch. OilGasAI LLC registered in Colorado. DJ Basin pilot deployments underway with four operators. Expanding to Permian Basin in 2027.
Technology

Sensor to compliance.
End to end.

Four integrated layers convert raw field readings into EPA-ready compliance reports — automatically.

01
🛰️
Field Sensor Layer
TGS2600 & TGS2611 MOx sensors + Airmar PB200 weather station + Raspberry Pi / Phidgets SBC4. Solar-powered, autonomous, cellular uplink. Deployed at wellpad perimeters.
Hardware
02
⚗️
ML Calibration
Random Forest regression corrects for temperature, humidity, cross-sensitivity, and baseline drift. Validated at METEC against Aeris MIRA reference analyzers. ±0.8 ppm accuracy.
Machine Learning
03
🧠
OilGasAI Model Alpha
Llama 3.1 70B fine-tuned on 21,429 domain pairs. QLoRA, 4-bit NF4. RAG pipeline with 37,009 EPA/PHMSA/OGMP 2.0 vectors. FastAPI backend. 0.847 RAG score on Subpart W queries.
LLM + RAG
04
📋
Compliance Output
Auto-generates EPA GHGRP Subpart W reports, NSPS OOOOa/b/c LDAR documentation, PHMSA integrity summaries, and OGMP 2.0 Level 1–5 quantification reports. CDX-ready.
